## Thursday, October 23, 2025

* Visit from Emily Cook for a research deep dive

* Research Deep Dive + Meaning Making
	* Select 1 of your items from your digital collection. 
	* Answer the following questions:
		* What is this?
		* Who made this? 
		* Why did they make it? What is its function when originally created?
		* When is it from?
		* How does it fit in the timeline of your topic?
		* How does it connect to your thesis?
		* What is its function for you now? 
		* What other primary sources can tell you about this item?
		* What secondary sources can help you understand/interpret this item?
